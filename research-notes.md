## Disclosure Context

Source reviewed: <https://guardiansmith.co.nz/disclaimer/> on 31 July 2026 (NZ time).

The existing disclosure page states that **Guardian Smith (2022) Limited (FSP1002543), trading as Guardian Smith, is the Financial Advice Provider**. It also provides the published information on lending scope, commissions and incentives, fees, duties, complaints, and privacy. The landing page should use a prominent **“Public Disclosure”** link pointing to this source rather than duplicating or paraphrasing regulated disclosure content.

Source reviewed: <https://www.fma.govt.nz/consumer/getting-advice/mortgage-advice/> (last updated 14 May 2026).

The Financial Markets Authority explains that mortgage advisers work with clients on needs, budget, options, product features and costs, and managing the process through settlement. It also emphasises questions about lender range, compensation, fees, recommendation rationale, loan features, and early-exit costs. Landing-page claims should therefore be outcome-neutral, focused on the advice process, and avoid guarantees of approvals, rates, savings, or lender decisions.

Source reviewed: <https://www.mbie.govt.nz/business-and-employment/business/financial-markets-conduct-regulation/financial-markets-conduct-act/regulation-of-financial-advice/regulations-to-support-the-financial-services-legislation-amendment-act/disclosure-requirements>.

The MBIE overview lists disclosure subjects including licensing, nature and scope of advice, fees/costs, commissions and conflicts, complaints/dispute resolution, and relevant disciplinary history. These details remain on the linked public disclosure page and are not recreated in page marketing copy.

## Implementation Decisions

- The initial contact form will request only name, contact method, and a high-level goal. It will not request sensitive financial details.
- The form will be presented as a conversation starter, not a loan application or advice process.
- The first release is a static prototype; a live submission endpoint or booking integration will require a backend or user-selected third-party form/booking destination.
- Any wording that makes a regulated claim, states fee arrangements, names lender panels, or changes professional information should be reviewed by Francis/Guardian Smith before publishing.
