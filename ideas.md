## Chosen Approach: The Financial Fitness Field Guide

### Design Movement

The page will combine **contemporary editorial design** with a restrained **sports-coaching field-guide aesthetic**. It is built around Francis’s genuine coaching background: a mortgage is not presented as a scary financial transaction, but as a well-prepared plan with a capable adviser doing the heavy lifting.

### Core Principles

1. **Reassuring momentum:** Each section should leave a visitor feeling that progress is practical and within reach.
2. **Expertise without stiffness:** Finance language stays clear and human, while the visual system remains polished enough to earn trust.
3. **Purposeful playfulness:** Graphic playbook markers, route lines, and small coaching cues add personality only where they clarify the journey.
4. **Conversion with low friction:** Calls to action are visible, precise, and repeated naturally throughout the page rather than overwhelming the visitor.

### Color Philosophy

The site will be anchored in a deep **ink-navy** that signals steadiness, a warm **paper-cream** that makes the page feel approachable and editorial, and an unmistakable **coach-green** for active moments. A soft **highlighter-yellow** will be used sparingly for annotations, route markers, and reassurance cues. The palette avoids cold corporate blue and generic fintech gradients; it should feel like a trusted strategy session on a well-used desk.

### Layout Paradigm

The page will read like a **progressive playbook**, not a conventional stack of centered blocks. A large asymmetric hero introduces Francis and the promise; angled route-lines and oversized numerals lead into a three-step process; service cards drift in alternating lanes; the booking panel becomes a prominent final “next move.” Mobile preserves the sequence as a vertical coaching path rather than simply shrinking a desktop grid.

### Signature Elements

1. **Playbook route-lines:** Fine dotted paths and numbered waypoints join key sections and reinforce the guided journey.
2. **The house-and-arrow mark:** A bold, geometric home outline with a forward route-arrow, used as the brand emblem and favicon.
3. **Field-note annotations:** Small green and yellow labels such as “Your next move” and “Straight answers, no jargon” add warmth and personality.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel encouraging and deliberate. Buttons respond with a small press and lift; service cards reveal a concise supporting note on hover; in-page navigation scrolls visitors to the relevant playbook step. Nothing should feel like a game or obscure financial information—motion supports clarity and confidence.

### Animation

On first view, route-lines draw in gently and key hero elements enter with a short upward fade. Card and button interactions use quick transform-and-shadow transitions of 160–220ms, with the active state compressing slightly. Section entrances are staggered at no more than 60ms. Motion is disabled or reduced for visitors who prefer reduced motion.

### Typography System

**DM Sans** will carry the readable body copy and practical interface language. **DM Serif Display** will give hero phrases, section numerals, and editorial moments a confident human cadence. Headline hierarchy uses tight, high-impact serif display text paired with emphatic sans-serif highlights; body text remains open, legible, and direct. Inter will not be used.

### Brand Essence

**That Mortgage Guy is the straight-talking New Zealand mortgage adviser for people who want a clear lending game plan and a calm expert in their corner.**

Personality: **encouraging, sharp, grounded**.

### Brand Voice

The voice is conversational, motivating, and plain-spoken. Headlines should sound like a capable coach, never a generic bank. CTAs invite a specific next move; microcopy removes pressure and reinforces clarity.

Example lines:

> “Your home move deserves a proper game plan.”

> “Bring the questions. Francis will bring the lender strategy.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark uses a sturdy sans-serif lockup with **Mortgage** highlighted in coach-green. The symbol is a simple angular house roof joined to a forward-pointing route-arrow, expressing property plus progress without relying on literal keys, dollar signs, or default banking icons.

### Signature Brand Color

**Coach Green — #1BAA68**

## Content and Compliance Guardrails

- Copy should describe financial guidance as a personalised conversation rather than promise an outcome, rate, approval, saving, or lender decision.
- The page will retain a clear link to the existing public disclosure statement and avoid unsupported claims about qualifications, licences, awards, review scores, or testimonials.
- The contact experience will ask only for information required to start a conversation and will state that submitting the form does not constitute loan approval or financial advice.
- Contact details and approved business/disclosure wording will use the existing public site as the source unless Francis supplies updates.

## Style Decisions

- The route-line, numbered waypoint, and field-note system will appear in every major section so the page reads as one continuous lending playbook rather than a series of separate marketing panels.
- The house-and-arrow mark will be treated as a signature navigation asset in the header, hero, process path, and final enquiry waypoint.
- Local home scenes and tactile planning imagery will carry the visual story; no synthetic likeness of Francis will be created. A real, approved Francis portrait can replace the planning visual if an original high-resolution file is supplied.
- The contact section is the playbook’s final practical step, so its form, supporting copy, and visual route cue will explicitly frame it as the visitor’s “next move.”
